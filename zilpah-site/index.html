The problem

You have a product people pay for. Outbound is the inconsistent part.

Pipeline depends on a few people

When one rep is busy or leaves, meetings dry up and nobody can say exactly why.

Tools without a system

Data providers, sequencers and a CRM are paid for, but they don’t add up to a repeatable process.

Hard to measure

It’s unclear which segments, messages and channels actually lead to qualified meetings and revenue.

How a pilot works

Start small, with success criteria agreed up front.

  1. Diagnose

    We review your customers, current outbound, tools and data to find where the process breaks.

  2. Define

    We agree on the target segment, what counts as a qualified meeting, and how results are measured.

  3. Build & run

    We set up the system in your tools and run it for a fixed period, with weekly check-ins.

  4. Review

    We compare results with the agreed criteria, then decide together whether to continue.

FAQ

Questions we often get.

Do you send cold email campaigns?

Not in Denmark. Danish law does not allow unsolicited marketing email to businesses without prior consent, so we focus on compliant channels such as professional networks, phone and inbound follow-up. Compliance with GDPR and the Danish Marketing Practices Act is part of the design from day one.

Do you replace our sales team or agency?

No. We build the system around the people you already have. Your team keeps the conversations and closes the deals.

How does pricing work?

You start with a pilot: a fixed-scope, fixed-price project that we quote after a first conversation. If it shows results, you can continue on a monthly retainer, with the option to link part of the fee to qualified meetings.

Which tools do you work with?

We build in the CRM and tools you already use, so the setup lives in accounts you own.

Where does the contact data come from?

From documented sources, with a lawful basis for contacting each person, recorded for every list we build.

What do you need from us?

Access to your CRM and outbound tools, time to walk us through your best customers, and someone to join the weekly check-ins and take the meetings.

Is this an AI tool?

No. We use AI and automation behind the scenes where they help, but what you get is a working process with measurable results, not another piece of software to learn.

Who is this for?

Growth-stage B2B SaaS companies with an established product and paying customers, starting in Denmark and the wider EU/EEA.
